# Member portal

Members manage their own membership from `/account`.

## What it does

- Members see their memberships, history, payments and receipts.
- They can renew, withdraw a renewal, leave, and ask for a refund.
- Their records are found by their verified email address.
- Where the organisation allows it, they upload their own card photo.
- They can download their data and ask for their details to be erased. Erasure anonymises the person, and money records are kept.

## Who can use it

Any member with a verified account. Staff review erasure requests with the `erasure.decide` permission.

## Turning it on or off

- Member accounts are a switch on the organisation. Off, members get emails that link to the public page or to a signed pay link instead.
- Member photo upload is a separate switch, off by default.
